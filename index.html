Daily Anchor

Navigating Kelsey

  • What She Sees in You She values your secure, visionary leadership. It makes her feel protected from chaos.
  • The Core Conflict You believe truth > comfort. She believes tone and harmony matter just as much. *Watch your delivery.*
  • Her Superpower She builds the atmosphere. She organizes life around you and remembers details you miss.

Navigating Jason

  • What He Needs He values directness, competence, and action. He wants to know *why* something matters logically.
  • The Core Conflict When he is blunt or impatient, he is often focused on *fixing the problem*, not dismissing your feelings.
  • His Superpower He builds the structure and future plans. He provides decisive leadership and stability.
